Training

The full spectrum of ways to train a model. Move down the stack as your workload matures. Every checkpoint deploys to production in seconds. Choose from the following:

  • •Get a guided path. Describe the task, review the plan and cost, approve the run, and get a trained model.
  • •Configuration-led. You know the model, data, and method. We handle scheduling, training, and the production handoff.
  • •Write your training logic. Your own loss, trainer, and RL loop, on our GPUs, rollout serving, and weight sync.
