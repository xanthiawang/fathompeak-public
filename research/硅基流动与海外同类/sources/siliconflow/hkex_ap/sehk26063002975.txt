[p1] OVERVIEW
We are a leading open, independent token supply platform in China, operating at the forefront of an
era defined by the rapid application of models across enterprises.
Our history dates back to August 2023 when Beijing SiliconFlow Technology Co., Ltd. (北京硅基
流動科技有限公司) (formerly known as Beijing SiliconFlow Technology Co., Ltd. (北京硅動科技有限公
司)) was established in the PRC. Since then, under the leadership of our co-founders, namely Dr. Yuan,
Mr. Liu Juncheng (柳俊丞), Mr. Zeng Hua (曾華), Ms. Zhao Zhen (趙震), and Mr. Hu Jian (胡健), we have
developed into a leading token supply platform in China. In June 2026, our Company was converted from
a limited liability company into a joint stock limited company and renamed to Beijing SiliconFlow
Technology Co., Ltd. (北京硅基流動科技股份有限公司).
