About Together AI

Together AI is a cloud platform for running open-source artificial intelligence models. It rents out Nvidia GPU clusters and provides tools for training, fine-tuning and deploying models such as DeepSeek, Llama and Kimi. The company makes money by charging usage-based fees for model inference and by leasing dedicated GPU clusters to enterprises and AI developers. It was co-founded by Vipul Ved Prakash, who sold his previous startup Topsy to Apple, along with a group of prominent AI researchers including FlashAttention creator Tri Dao.
