## Distinctive (only 1–2 have it)
- **Time-of-day pricing:** only DeepSeek (off-peak at 50%) and SiliconFlow (02:00–08:00 window). Neither ties it to energy.
- **Public energy proof:** only Crusoe (annual impact reports, climate risk report) and Soluna (live MWh counter, white paper). Emerald adds peer-reviewed grid-flex trials. **No token seller links its token price or its reports to the energy source.**
- **Usage rankings / compare / State of AI:** OpenRouter only.
- **Cost calculator:** Together (PTU) only. **Startup credits:** Together only.
- **Status page:** none of the 3 Chinese platforms has one. All 4 US developer platforms do.
- **Certifications / trust center:** US platforms only. Chinese sites show filings instead; PPIO shows 网信算备.
- **Team seats + per-key budgets:** PPIO Token Plan, OpenRouter.
- **Partner program with a research track:** SiliconFlow.
- **Agent-tool integration guides (Claude Code, Codex…):** DeepSeek.
- **Individual sign-up closed:** Infinigence (enterprise only).
