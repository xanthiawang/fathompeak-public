## 1 自主运营
EN: Independent operation

标签：自主运营
EN: Independent operation

标题：在你的场站部署算电世界模型
EN: Deploy a power–compute world model at your site

正文：沐仞将算电世界模型与一体机部署到你的场站。你自己接单运营，沐仞负责部署、模型适配与远程支持。
EN: FathomPeak deploys the power–compute world model and appliances at your site. You manage orders and operations; FathomPeak handles deployment, model adaptation, and remote support.

## 2 新增核心优势卡
EN: New core capability card

标题：算电世界模型部署到场站
EN: On-site deployment of the power–compute world model

引言：专做算电调度的小模型，部署到你的场站。使用你自己的数据，数据留在场站，模型按日用调度结果更新。
EN: A small model dedicated to power–compute scheduling is deployed at your site. It uses your own data, keeps that data on-site, and updates daily using scheduling results.

- 接入场站出力、电价与单卡功耗
EN: Integrate site power output, electricity prices, and per-card power consumption.
- 接入订单、报价与交付要求
EN: Integrate orders, quotes, and delivery requirements.
- 将执行后的用电与减排计量回写模型
EN: Feed measured electricity use and emissions reductions back into the model after execution.

## 3 改写核心优势第一张卡
EN: Revised first core capability card

标题：按用电与交付要求安排任务
EN: Schedule tasks around power conditions and delivery requirements

引言：比较任务在不同时段、设备上运行的用电与成本。选定排法后，经 Tapestrix 下发到一体机执行。
EN: Compare electricity use and costs for running tasks at different times and on different devices. Once a schedule is selected, Tapestrix dispatches it to the appliances for execution.

- 结合场站出力与电价安排运行时段
EN: Schedule runs based on site power output and electricity prices.
- 按单卡功耗与订单交付要求安排设备
EN: Assign devices based on per-card power consumption and order delivery requirements.
- 对比不同排法的用电与成本推演结果
EN: Compare projected electricity use and costs across alternative schedules.

## 4 替换行业价值
EN: Replacement industry value statement

标题：场站自己的调度模型
EN: Your site’s own scheduling model

正文：用场站自己的数据持续更新，让任务安排依据本场站的出力、电价与运行结果。
EN: Keep the model updated with your site’s own data, so task scheduling reflects local power output, electricity prices, and operating results.
