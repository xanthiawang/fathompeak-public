## 1. 核心痛点
EN: Core challenges

**调用增长，预算难控**
EN: Growing usage, unpredictable spend  
用量随业务扩张，费用难预估，也难对应业务收益。（依据：IDC 中国 2026-03；Ramp 2026-04）
EN: As usage grows, costs become harder to forecast and link to business returns. (Sources: IDC China, March 2026; Ramp, April 2026)

**自建算力，闲置也贵**
EN: Idle capacity adds to ownership costs  
业务负载有高有低，自建算力仍需承担闲置成本。（依据：arXiv:2606.11690；arXiv:2607.13080）
EN: Business demand fluctuates, but owned infrastructure continues to incur costs when idle. (Sources: arXiv:2606.11690; arXiv:2607.13080)

**敏感数据，部署两难**
EN: Sensitive data complicates deployment  
业务希望尽快用上模型，数据保护与治理要求也需兼顾。（依据：Code Ninety 2026；Gallagher 2026）
EN: Teams want to adopt AI quickly while meeting data protection and governance requirements. (Sources: Code Ninety, 2026; Gallagher, 2026)

**用模排放，核算缺据**
EN: AI emissions lack a clear accounting basis  
按金额估算难反映实际用电，排放披露缺少可靠依据。（依据：arXiv:2608.06733；arXiv:2606.10660）
EN: Spend-based estimates may not reflect actual electricity use, leaving emissions disclosures without reliable evidence. (Sources: arXiv:2608.06733; arXiv:2606.10660)

**跨境调用，链路难查**
EN: Cross-border access is hard to verify  
跨境调用涉及多重合规要求，模型来源与计费也需核实。（依据：中伦相关文章；同花顺 2026-07）
EN: Cross-border access involves overlapping compliance requirements, alongside model provenance and billing checks. (Sources: Zhong Lun; 10jqka, July 2026)

## 2. 核心优势
EN: Our capabilities

### 用量账单归集到业务（对应痛点 1）
EN: Connect usage and billing to business activity (Addresses challenge 1)

按 Token 实际用量计费。通过组织钱包与逐单记录，让费用有归属、用量可核对。
EN: Pay for actual token usage. Organization wallets and per-order records make spending attributable and usage verifiable.

- 正式报价前完成业务用量测试
  EN: Test workload usage before issuing a formal quote.
- 通过主子账号归集组织用量
  EN: Consolidate organizational usage through parent and sub-accounts.
- 提供逐单用量与计费核对记录
  EN: Provide per-order usage and billing records for reconciliation.

### 算电调度降低用模成本（对应痛点 1、2）
EN: Reduce model costs through power–compute scheduling (Addresses challenges 1 and 2)

批量任务可排入绿电时段，公开价 4 折起。按量购买 Token，无需先投入自建算力。
EN: Schedule batch workloads during green-power periods, with pricing starting at 40% of public rates. Buy tokens as needed without upfront investment in owned compute.

- 将批量任务安排至绿电时段
  EN: Schedule batch workloads during green-power periods.
- 按业务实际用量提供模型服务
  EN: Supply model services based on actual business usage.
- 根据业务测试结果提供报价
  EN: Quote based on workload test results.

### 按业务边界选择部署（对应痛点 3、5）
EN: Choose deployment to fit business requirements (Addresses challenges 3 and 5)

敏感业务可选私有化部署。跨境模型调用提供获批通道，模型与计费逐单可查。
EN: Private deployment is available for sensitive workloads. Approved channels support cross-border model access, with model and billing details traceable for each order.

- 提供私有化一体机与远程运维
  EN: Provide a private-deployment appliance with remote operations support.
- 提供持网信办批复的跨境通道
  EN: Provide cross-border channels approved by the Cyberspace Administration of China.
- 逐单记录调用模型与计费明细
  EN: Record the model used and billing details for each order.

### 逐单提供用电减排依据（对应痛点 4）
EN: Provide energy and emissions-reduction evidence per order (Addresses challenge 4)

产地卡记录每单实测用电量与减排量。企业核算有据可查，减少对金额估算的依赖。
EN: Each origin card records measured electricity use and emissions reductions for an order. These records support accounting with less reliance on spend-based estimates.

- 随订单提供可查验的产地卡
  EN: Provide a verifiable origin card with each order.
- 逐单记录任务的实测用电量
  EN: Record measured electricity use for each order.
- 提供每单对应的减排量记录
  EN: Provide emissions-reduction records for each order.
