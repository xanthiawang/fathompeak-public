## 核心痛点

- **风光出力难以消纳**：风光利用率持续走低，弃电时段增加，三北场站的消纳压力更突出。
  EN: **More Power Goes Unused**: Declining wind and solar utilization leaves more generation curtailed, especially across northern China.
- **午间发电收入承压**：午间光伏大发时，现货电价探底甚至为负，多发电也未必增加收入。
  EN: **Midday Generation Earns Less**: Spot prices can fall to zero or below during peak solar hours, so more generation may not mean more revenue.
- **电站收益更难预判**：新能源全面入市后，收入随市场电价波动，新增项目还需参与竞价。
  EN: **Revenue Becomes Harder to Forecast**: Market trading exposes renewable revenue to fluctuating electricity prices, while new projects face competitive bidding.
- **电力外送面临约束**：外送线路建设成本高，绿电直连上网比例受限，富余电量难以送出。
  EN: **Exporting Surplus Power Is Difficult**: Transmission lines are costly to build, and direct green power projects face limits on grid exports.

## 行业价值

- **富余电力就地利用**：一体机在场站将弃电时段的电转为 Token，无需新增并网资产或占用外送通道。
  EN: **Put Surplus Power to Work On-Site**: The appliance turns otherwise curtailed power into Token output on-site, without additional grid-connected assets or transmission capacity.
- **对接绿色算力需求**：将场站富余绿电用于算力生产，为关注用电成本的 AI 客户提供绿色算力。
  EN: **Serve Demand for Affordable Green Compute**: Use surplus renewable power to supply green compute to AI customers seeking lower electricity costs.
- **绿电来源逐单可查**：每单产地卡记录电站、时段、用电量与减排量，供买家用于 ESG 披露和绿色采购。
  EN: **Trace Green Power by Order**: Each origin card records the power station, usage period, electricity consumed, and emissions reductions for ESG disclosure and green procurement.
- **按现有资源选择投入**：可出设备联合运营、仅提供场地与电力，或将既有算力接入。
  EN: **Choose How You Participate**: Supply equipment for joint operations, contribute site space and power, or connect existing compute capacity.
